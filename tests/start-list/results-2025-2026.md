Generated by `tests/start-list/benchmark.py`: each race's published entries (categories, clubs, courses, first controls, starts) run through `build_start_list.py` with the default rules (automatic window, compact pace, no rankings, `random_seed` 2027), checked by `validate.py` and compared with the exact shortest window from `exact_window.py` (CP-SAT, 60 s per race, up to 180 s for the largest). Minutes are window lengths (last minute of the grid). An optimum is proven by CP-SAT or when it equals a simple lower bound (busiest first control, load, longest category); otherwise the Exact column gives the proven range. The exact model may load every minute up to `max_load` (6), while the script's automatic window keeps the pace at 5.5 starters a minute on average (4.5 with 3 or more starts): Pace floor is the shortest window that pace allows, so a gap down to it is by design.
